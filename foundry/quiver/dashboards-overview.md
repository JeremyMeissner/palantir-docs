Dashboards. Quiver dashboard mode allows you to present insights from your analysis in read-only, interactive dashboards. Dashboards can be used as standalone views or embedded in other Foundry applications. Key features. Create multiple dashboards per analysis. Start from a blank dashboard and easily drag and drop content from your analysis into the dashboard. Choose between three view styles to optimize your dashboard for standalone consumption or for embedding in other applications. Embed dashboards in other parts of Foundry, including Workshop modules, Notepad documents, Carbon workspaces, and Object Views.
