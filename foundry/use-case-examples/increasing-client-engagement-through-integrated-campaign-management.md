# Increasing client engagement through integrated campaign management

Industry Sector: Health Care. Business Function: Customer Relations. Palantir Foundry can be used to segment members based on specific characteristics, orchestrate the end-to-end campaign management lifecycle, and propose next best actions according to the member's risk profile and responses. One campaign sent over 100M messages to members leading to a 1.6X increase in vaccination rates. Challenge. Member engagement was managed within markets to account for state- and product-level restrictions, leading to an inability to deploy enterprise-wide engagement strategies or learn and improve on best practices. Solution. Dynamic targeting across member characteristics: fully integrated Member view enables dynamic targeting based on demographics, medical claims, prescriptions, socioeconomic factors, etc. to identify member cohorts for engagement. End-to-end campaign management: coordination of multi-channel campaigns with progress tracking against specific success KPIs and member behavior targets to inform future campaign strategies. Next Best Action proposals: proactively surface opportunities for engagement based on claims, electronic health records, and Rx adherence, among other inputs. Users and stakeholders. Member Orchestration. Impact. Over 100M messages sent in first enterprise-wide campaign. 1.6X increase in COVID-19 vaccination rate for contacted members. Implement a similar use case.
