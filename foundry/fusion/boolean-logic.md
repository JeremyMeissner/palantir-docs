Boolean logic. You can give a cell a Boolean value by entering =true or =false. You can also do basic Boolean operations in Fusion such as: =if((A1 == 'x') OR NOT ('x' == 'y'), 'true', 'false'). Note that Fusion requires the use of single quotes ('), not double quotes (").
