# Data Lineage

Data Lineage is an interactive tool that facilitates a holistic view of how data flows through the Foundry platform. With Data Lineage, you can: Easily find and discover datasets Search to find datasets using Project, table, and column names. Click through Foundry Projects to browse data. Explore pipelines through a powerful interface Expand or hide ancestors and descendants of datasets. View attributes of a group of tables at once. Visualize your pipeline through coloring (e.g. color out-of-date tables). Drill into details about your data such as its schema, when it was last built, and the code that generated the data itself. Collaborate with teammates Create pipeline snapshots to share with other users.
