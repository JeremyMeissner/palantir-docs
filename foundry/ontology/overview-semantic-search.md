Semantic search. Semantic search is a way to search for text based on the inherent meaning or context, rather than relying solely on keywords or other traditional search methods. Semantic search is accomplished using AI models to transform the text into vectors, which are arrays of numbers, and are called "embeddings". If the model is effective, the vectors, each of size N, that are close to each other in N-dimensional space are the ones that have similar underlying or semantic meaning. For example, the embedding vector of “face mask” will be closer to the embedding vector of “face covering” than it is to “respirator.” If the embedded text is then associated with a particular object in the Ontology, then your search-driven operational workflows become much more useful. Finding related entities or entities related to a particular search query is simply finding the nearest vectors in N-dimensional space.
