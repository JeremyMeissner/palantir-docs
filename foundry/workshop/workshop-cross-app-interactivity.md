Cross-application interactivity. Workshop supports all methods of cross-application interactivity to enable data sharing across distinct applications. Use drag and drop and the App Pairing widget to integrate your Workshop module with and connect it to other Palantir applications. Commands enable you to execute actions and operations across applications independent of or in tandem with the App Pairing widget. For Workshop-specific guidance on configuring drag zones and drop zones, see Drag and drop in Workshop. Learn more about cross-application interactivity.
