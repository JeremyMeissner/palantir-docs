# Review models

Reviews on models allow various stakeholders from different backgrounds to collaborate on model evaluation, and set up processes for holistic release decisions. Stakeholders may include modeling peers, modeling leads, organizational owners, subject-matter experts (SMEs), internal governance or ethics boards, and more. Objective owners can set roles to control access for review, release, and deployment.
