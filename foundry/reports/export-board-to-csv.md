# Export a board's data to CSV

The Foundry Reports application has been sunsetted, and legacy reports are now available as view-only. Refer to the Reports sunset announcement for further details. The data that populates a Contour board can be exported to CSV. Click Show data in the Contour board’s footer Click Export to CSV in the header of the table fly out Wait for the export to complete.
